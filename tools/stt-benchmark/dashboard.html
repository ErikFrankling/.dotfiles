Speed and cost are separate from quality

End-to-end elapsed time includes different loading, network and chunking costs. These are observed runs, not a controlled hardware benchmark. Real-time factor 0.1 means processing takes one tenth of the recording duration.

Each point is one complete three-clip configuration. Horizontal: elapsed ÷ audio duration, lower is faster. Vertical: word disagreement, lower is closer to Codex. Outliers above 50% are labeled in the agreement chart and omitted here.
